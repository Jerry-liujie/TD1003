\label{chap:related}

% TODO(revision): replace this placeholder introduction once the

This chapter surveys the work most closely related to this
dissertation. The three systems presented in
Chapters~\ref{slab:chap}--\ref{dag:chap} draw on, and depart from,
several bodies of prior work: rule-based and synthesis-based query
rewriting, applications of large language models to database tasks,
and the optimization of multi-query SQL pipelines. We organize the
discussion around these themes.

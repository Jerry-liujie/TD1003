\section{Conclusion}
\label{slab:sec:conc}

This chapter answered the question it opened with: whole-query
rewriting does work with no rules at all. \slabcity demonstrates it,
optimizing up to 1.7x more queries than state-of-the-art rewriters and
producing outputs up to four orders of magnitude faster, and the
ablations locate the reason in the chapter's central idea: dataflow
guidance is what makes the rule-free search tractable, and without it
the search finds nothing in its budget. Query rewriting sits upstream
of, and composes with, traditional plan-level optimization and tuning;
a well-rewritten query is a better starting point for everything the
optimizer does afterward. Extensions of this framework, including
using synthesized rewrites to generalize and discover new rewrite
rules, are discussed with the other future directions in
\cref{chap:future}.

The search, however, has a ceiling of its own. Dataflow guidance is
structural: as queries grow, their dataflows multiply, candidate
combinations explode combinatorially, and vast numbers of candidates
collapse onto the same score, leaving the search without direction
precisely where direction is most needed. The next chapter takes up
this failure mode, and asks whether the knowledge that rules tried to
encode by hand, and search tried to do without, can instead be
learned.
